# A New Draft Post

Drafts and ideas that are quick and easy to find. The concept I have realised with blog posts and websites. Most sites and blogs you create will never be seen by the people you want to see it. Thats ok as its a great way to practise writing and documenting work so that others (and yourself) can come back to it in the future and understand how it all works.
