I am a PhD student in Accounting at Yale University, where I study empirical accounting with a focus on capital markets and firm valuations.

Before Yale, I studied Quantitative Econometrics at University of Texas at Austin and worked across Dallas, Hong Kong, and Shanghai — first as a CPA at Deloitte, then in an investment role at a U.S. dollar fund. Those years gave me a front-row seat to how data, judgment, and institutions shape financial decisions, and they quietly set me on the path toward research.

My goal is to become a researcher and educator who uses data-driven insights to improve financial decision-making and empower future generations.

I am only getting started.
