# Title: Chasing variance

Is model selection driven by chance? How to tell when working with mixed hierarical models with more than one level. 

# Overview

Estimation and prediction are two different questions, needing to be addressed in somewhat opposing angles.

## Application

Example 1: Invasive predator control and decisions about target species dependent on these sorts of models (this could give us a strong example of the impacts).

Example 2: Could look at the citizen science data and mixed models for estimating koala abundance based on different monitoring methods (this could be easier but less impact) and the ability to detect changes in true abundance using such fuzzy methods and mixed models.
